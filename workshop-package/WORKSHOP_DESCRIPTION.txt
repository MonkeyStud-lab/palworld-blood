Blood splatter for human NPCs and Pals.

Effects
- Humans: hit blood spray + ground decals, death pools, headshot decapitation
- Pals: death blood (pool + death spray); no per-hit FX (stability)
- Player: no blood FX

Requirements
- UE4SS Experimental (Palworld) workshop dependency (UE4SSExperimentalPW)

Important
- Do NOT use together with the original Blood Splatter mod (petyr1710). Unsubscribe/disable the original to avoid double FX/crashes.
- Client-side visuals only. Mature/gore content.
- v1.0.3: combat stability hotfix (FX deferred off damage hook; pals death-only)
