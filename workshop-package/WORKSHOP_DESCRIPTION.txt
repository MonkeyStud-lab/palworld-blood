Blood splatter for human NPCs and Pals.

Effects
- Humans: ground blood decals, death pools, headshot decapitation
- Pals: hit blood (throttled) + death pools — boss pals (_BOSS) skipped for stability
- Player: no blood FX

Requirements
- UE4SS Experimental (Palworld) workshop dependency (UE4SSExperimentalPW)

Important
- Do NOT use together with the original Blood Splatter mod (petyr1710). Unsubscribe/disable the original to avoid double FX/crashes.
- Client-side visuals only. Mature/gore content.
- v1.0.6: skip blood FX on boss pals (_BOSS) to reduce UE4SS combat crashes; hit Niagara/neck geyser remain off
